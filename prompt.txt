Build a web app called "Research Paper Agent" using React.

The app should let a user enter a research topic and then:
1. Search for and list relevant papers (title, authors, year, short summary)
2. Summarize a selected paper in plain language
3. Suggest 3 follow-up research questions

Think step by step: first identify key concepts in the topic, then
find related papers, then summarize, then suggest follow-ups.

Example 1:
Input: "transformer models in NLP"
Output: Key concepts: attention, encoder-decoder. Paper: "Attention Is
All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017) - introduced the transformer
architecture. Follow-ups: How do transformers scale with data size?

Example 2:
Input: "climate change and crop yields"
Output: Key concepts: temperature stress, CO2 fertilization. Paper:
"Temperature increase reduces global yields of major crops" (Zhao et
al., 2017). Follow-ups: Which regions are most vulnerable?

Use the Gemini API for the AI features. Use a clean, simple UI.
